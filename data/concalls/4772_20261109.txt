0 5   C O R E  T E C H N O L O G Y  &  C O M P E T I T I V E N E S S
Product Specifications
TSC has complete technologies of synthesis, purification, transfilling, and quality analysis 
of silane products, and is one of the four global manufacturers that can supply 
semiconductor grade disilane.
TSC owns the largest capacity of a single disilane manufacturing line in the world.
TSC is one of the two global manufacturers possessing technologies of trisilane.
 TSC's new product – anhydrous HF is a monopolized SEG by foreign suppliers.
Silane
SiH4 ·  Silane  ·  甲硅烷
• Commonly utilized as raw material for 
the Si-thin film deposition in optical 
display panel fabrication, and also 
applied in the Si-based epitaxial thin film 
in mature semiconductor fabrication 
processes.
Disilane
Si2H6 ·  Disilane  ·  乙硅烷
• Applied in advanced semiconductor 
fabrication process for epitaxy dielectric 
Si-based thin film on electronic circuits.
• Better application properties than silane 
in the formation of thin film density, 
surface roughness, deposition rate, and 
thermal budget allowance.
Trisilane
Si3H8 ·  Trisilane  ·  丙硅烷
• Properties and applications are similar to 
the lower silanes.
• Possesses better application properties, 
and is considered to be the key Si-based 
epitaxial raw material of the next 
generation.
• Actual application in one of the Tier 1 
foundry company's fabrication.
Anhydrous HF
AHF  ·  Anhydrous hydrogen fluoride
• HF is an important SEG to advanced 
semiconductor dry etching process and 
tool cleaning, mainly for thin film layer 
etching, CVD tool chamber cleaning and 
silicon deposits removal.
• AHF provides better traits of volatility 
and isotropy compared to HF solution 
for application in foundry processes of 
finer nodes.

Chemical Automation
Automated Acid Supply System
Safety: Automated acid supply and discharge 
greatly reduce the risk of chemical leakage 
and personnel exposure.
Precise control: Metered automatic dosing 
reduces waste liquid and chemical waste.
Green operations: Higher acid utilization 
efficiency, in line with environmental (E) and 
occupational safety (S) standards.
Handling Automation
Self-propelled Electric Acid 
Drum Handler
Zero-carbon handling: Fully electric drive 
enables zero-emission in-plant logistics.
Ergonomics: Replaces manual handling of 
heavy chemical drums, eliminating 
musculoskeletal strain risks.
Smart safety: Automatic clamping and stable 
transport reduce spill hazards caused by 
sudden shaking.
Process Robotics
Automated Physical Polishing 
Robot Arm

dedicated polishing pads significantly 
improves processing yield and consistency.

and physical harm.
